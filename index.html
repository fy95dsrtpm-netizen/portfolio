I started actively doing street photography in 2023.

Inspired by Georgy Pinkhassov and Robert Capa, I became interested in photography as a way of observing life: quiet moments, movement, light, and the fragile atmosphere of the street.

Currently living in Russia, I photograph everyday life as it unfolds naturally.
